### Rationale
This branch (`feat/source-lan-tree-ai-chat`) attempted an aggressive reduction from 43 routes down to 15 and replaced the multi-stage architecture with a simplified tabbed interface. In doing so, it oversimplified the system and lost track of the primary purpose:
1. Generalized multi-device LAN & Cloud awareness (`laptop`, `debian1`, `xiaomi-mobile`, `Google Drive`).
2. Deep, recursive subfolder profiling (instead of superficial path autocompletes).
3. Human-in-the-Middle natural language rule synthesis and outlier exception handling.
4. "Von → Nach" Tree-Diff visualization of the self-healing ideal tree ($S_{ideal}$).

All future development continues on `feat/lan-tree-diff-organizer` branched directly from `main`.
